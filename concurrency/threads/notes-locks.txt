Concurrency versus Parallelism

Concurrency: structuring a program so tasks can be in progress at overlapping times, interleaving
Parallelism: running at same time on different cores

Atomic: an operation guaranteed to complete as one step, no lock and a single machine instruction
Semaphore: counter initialized to N, acquire decrements, release increments, block at zero
Mutex: mutual exclusion and when multiple states are being updated, notion of ownership where same thread locks/unlocks
